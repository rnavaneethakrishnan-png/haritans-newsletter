Robots are learning the job inside the factory

Boston Dynamics opened its Robotics Metaplant Application Center inside Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America. Atlas robots are being trained to sequence automotive parts for assembly; heavier and more repetitive tasks are planned as the programme grows.

2Learn with people

Our POV: the breakthrough is not a human-shaped machine. It is the training loop around it. Mechanical design, controls, shop-floor knowledge and AI now have to improve together.

Employee Update

Update on PF Contribution Sep 2026

From 17 September 2026, the EPF wage ceiling increased from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month under Notification S.O. 5109(E). For employees contributing at the revised statutory ceiling, the monthly employee PF contribution moves from ₹1,800 to ₹3,000, strengthening long-term PF savings and family social security.

The change applies prospectively from 17 September 2026.

Proprioception
pro-pree-oh-SEP-shun
MeaningYour body's hidden sense of where its parts are and how they are moving, even when you cannot see them.
In a sentence“Artificial proprioception helps a humanoid robot place its feet and maintain balance without constantly looking down.”Try it: close your eyes and touch your nose. That is proprioception at work.

Gear Logic

Gear A has 12 teeth and drives Gear B with 36 teeth. If Gear A turns 3 full rotations, how many rotations does Gear B make?

Gear B makes 1 rotation in the opposite direction. A 3:1 tooth ratio trades speed for torque.

EV Range Check

An EV starts at 80 percent charge. It uses 12 percentage points every 50 km. After 250 km, what charge remains?

3. Wrist Reset
