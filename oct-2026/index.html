October Tech Signal

The robot is learning to decide where the work happens

A 6 October announcement puts Physical AI directly into two demanding factory problems: collaborative robotics and intelligent welding.

ON-DEVICE
6 October 2026 | Physical AI

From programmed motion to local judgement

Doosan Robotics was selected for two Korean government-backed R&D projects: a next-generation collaborative robot using on-device AI semiconductors, and an intelligent welding system for complex industrial work.

The ambition is important: perception, decision-making and control move closer to the robot instead of depending entirely on a distant cloud.

Our POV: the next productivity leap will not come from making machines move faster. It will come from helping them understand variation while engineers define the boundaries of safe action.

Reference: Doosan Group press release
Engineering Explainer

Why intelligence at the edge changes the factory

A robot that can sense and respond locally gains something factories value deeply: useful speed without waiting for a round trip to the cloud.

01

Sense

Vision, force and position sensors capture what is actually happening, not what the plan assumed.

02

Interpret

An on-device model identifies the weld seam, component variation or unexpected obstruction.

03

Decide

The controller selects an approved response within safety and quality limits defined by engineers.

04

Act

The machine adjusts its path or pauses immediately, while valuable learning can still be shared upstream.

What changes for engineers?More time moves from repeating adjustments to designing constraints, validating edge cases and improving the system.
What changes for people?Hazardous and repetitive work can shift toward machines while human expertise remains responsible for intent, judgement and escalation.
Word of the Month

Affordance

uh-FOR-dns
Meaning

A quality of an object or environment that suggests what actions are possible: a handle affords pulling; a button affords pressing.

In engineering

“The cobot recognised the handle's affordance and selected a safe gripping motion.”

Why it matters

Physical AI must learn more than object names. It must infer what can be done, where, and under which constraints.

Torque Trade

Gear A drives Gear B. If A rotates six times, how many turns does B make, and in which direction?

Two turns, in the opposite direction. The 3:1 ratio reduces speed and increases available torque.

Sensor Vote

Three independent sensors report SAFE, SAFE and UNSAFE. A two-out-of-three rule is used. What does the controller decide?

SAFE. The majority rule tolerates one conflicting reading, though a safety-critical system may still log or investigate it.

Weld Path

A robot completes four equal welds. Each weld takes 18 seconds and each reposition takes 4 seconds. How long from the first weld start to the last weld finish?

84 seconds. Four welds take 72 seconds, with three reposition moves adding 12 seconds.
